# H-004 — A complete baseline-optimizer transplant closes the normalized-legacy deficit


## Provenance
Prospective bounded intervention; [X-002](../explorations/X-002-optimization-and-regime.md).

## Claim and regime
At fixed normalized-legacy coordinates, a from-start complete baseline optimizer/config
mapping yields an epoch50 score within 0.2pp of its matched baseline control and above
the audited normalized-legacy/legacy-optimizer reference. Hold all other scientific
factors fixed or explicitly map them as required by the existing identity control.

## Support / falsification / inconclusive
Support requires the mapped-control comparison and a genuinely matched legacy-optimizer
reference. If the latter is unavailable, only H-002 is tested; do not attribute a
historical unmatched score difference to the optimizer. A qualified remaining deficit
outside the proposed 0.2pp margin contradicts sufficiency of this intervention. The
margin is proposed and must be frozen before execution. A positive result establishes
sufficiency for this bundled mapping, not the unique responsible LR or a global optimum.
