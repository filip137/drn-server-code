# Campaign instructions

Read the root AGENTS.md and .agents/skills/experiment-loop/SKILL.md. Follow its
role-specific reading: begin with the assigned pilot/experiment note and open additional
context only for the current decision. Reuse unchanged context across role transitions.
Campaigns organize research; the existing runner, execution policy and canonical
run-bundle contract remain in force. Keep execution state and result paths in the
owning experiment/pilot note, and conclusions in its result/pilot note.

For the two seeded questions, put all new planning here. Preserve old plans and results
at their original paths as provenance; do not reactivate their expired launch queues.
Keep MNIST endpoint-noise EqProp and CIFAR blockwise BPTT as separate evidence regimes.
Do not transfer a beta, solver qualification or scheme ranking between them by analogy.

Respect root protections for docs/current_state.md and docs/my_notes.md.
Do not generate scientific interpretation into the human-authored dashboard.

Use stable campaign-local IDs and record negative results, retrospective hypotheses,
missing source evidence and proposed decision thresholds. Never invent a completed run,
validation pass, config hash or launch authorization. Small pilots use the skill's
single-note convention in `campaigns/pilots/` without campaign scaffolding or a ledger.
Reuse existing assigned pilot notes in place. Existing full campaigns
retain their structure; only ledger.md is generated. Check affected full-campaign ledgers
after metadata/result changes; run generator tests when its code changes. Structural
validation is not evidence validation. Keep tool output and handoffs to selected facts
and paths rather than full metadata dumps.
